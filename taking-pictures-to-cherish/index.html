How to Take Pictures You’ll Be Glad You Took

Maria Vernon

For early childhood teachers, taking photos is part of the job. Over decades in the classroom, I’ve taken  thousands of photographs to illustrate children’s Learning Stories, which help parents stay connected to their child’s growth, relationships, and everyday learning at the center.

So I’ve worked to make my photos special.

To move from a quick snapshot to a photograph that truly sparkles (and has parents asking for a copy!) doesn’t require fancy equipment or technical know-how. It comes down to a few simple techniques that anyone can learn and use.

My free guide, Taking Photos to Cherish, shares the practical skills I found worked for me. Take these four simple, easy to remember steps and you’ll capture images that feel real, tell a story, and are genuinely worth keeping.
