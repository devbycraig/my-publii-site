Why You Should Print Family Photos...

Maria Vernon

Today's children are the most photographed generation in history.

They have the least access.

In bygone years, photos were special. They were shared and displayed.

Today photos are locked on smartphones and require adult permission and passwords to view.

Photographs show a family as an extended unit—grandparents, uncles, aunties, cousins—and they help children see themselves as an important and valued part. Photographs of wider family members help young children feel a sense of security and belonging.

My free guide, Staying in the Picture, is packed with 12 creative, hands-on suggestions to get family photos off your phone and into your child's hands. Whether it’s creating a photo play cube, making laminated magnets, or building a “super-quick” album, these craft ideas are designed to be played with and loved by your child.
